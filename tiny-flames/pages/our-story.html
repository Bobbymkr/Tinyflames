Our Story

From a small kitchen experiment to lighting up homes across the country.

The Beginning

Tiny Flames started in 2018 when our founder, Sarah Chen, couldn't find a candle that truly captured the warmth of her grandmother's home. Frustrated by mass-produced options filled with synthetic fragrances and parabens, she decided to create her own.

What began as weekend experiments in her Brooklyn kitchen quickly turned into a passion project. After months of perfecting her soy wax blend and sourcing natural essential oils, Sarah poured her first batch of candles for friends and family. The response was overwhelming.

By 2019, Tiny Flames had grown from kitchen batches to a small studio in Queens, where a team of three artisans hand-poured every single candle. Today, we're proud to serve thousands of customers who value quality, customization, and sustainability.

Natural Ingredients

100% soy wax, cotton wicks, and premium fragrance oils. Never parabens, phthalates, or synthetic dyes.

Hand-Poured

Every candle is crafted by hand in small batches to ensure quality and attention to detail.

Cruelty-Free

We never test on animals and are certified by Leaping Bunny.

Sustainable

Eco-friendly packaging and a candle recycling program to minimize our environmental impact.

Why Customization Matters

We believe that candles are more than just home decor—they're memories waiting to be created. That's why we pioneered our custom label program, allowing you to personalize every candle for birthdays, weddings, corporate events, or just because.

Whether it's adding a loved one's name, a special date, or a meaningful message, we want your Tiny Flames candle to tell your story. Over 50,000 custom candles have been created since we launched this feature in 2020.
