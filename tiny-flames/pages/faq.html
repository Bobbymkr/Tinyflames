Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and policies.

Orders & Shipping

How long does processing take before shipping?

Most orders are processed and shipped within 1-2 business days. Custom candles may take an additional 1-2 days for personalization. During peak seasons (November-December), processing may take up to 3 business days.

What are your shipping options and costs?

We offer Standard Shipping (3-5 business days, $5.95), Express Shipping (1-2 business days, $12.95), and Overnight Shipping ($24.95). Free standard shipping is available on orders over $50 within the continental US.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to Canada, UK, Australia, and select European countries. International shipping rates vary by destination and are calculated at checkout. Delivery typically takes 7-14 business days. Customs fees may apply.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also log into your account or visit our Track Order page and enter your order number and email address.

What if my package arrives damaged?

We're sorry if this happens! Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We'll send a replacement immediately at no cost to you.

Products & Customization

What type of wax do you use?

All our candles are made with 100% natural soy wax, which is renewable, biodegradable, and burns cleaner than paraffin. Our wax is sourced from American-grown soybeans.

Are your fragrances natural?

We use a blend of essential oils and premium phthalate-free fragrance oils. All our scents are carefully tested to ensure they're safe and non-toxic when burned properly.

How do I customize a candle?

Visit our Customize page to choose your scent, size, lid color, and add personalized text for the label. You can add a name (up to 20 characters) and a short message (up to 30 characters).

What's the burn time for each size?

Our 8oz candles burn for approximately 45 hours, while our 12oz candles burn for about 70 hours. For best results, trim the wick to 1/4 inch before each burn and allow the wax to melt evenly across the surface.

Can I reorder a custom candle I previously purchased?

Yes! If you have an account, your custom designs are saved in your order history. Simply find the previous order and click "Reorder" to recreate your custom candle.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unused candles in their original packaging. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the return.

Can I return a custom candle?

Due to their personalized nature, custom candles are final sale unless they arrive damaged or defective. If there's an error on our part, we'll happily replace it at no cost.

How do I initiate a return?

Log into your account and go to Order History, or contact our support team with your order number. We'll provide a prepaid return label and instructions.

Do you offer exchanges?

Yes! If you'd like to exchange a candle for a different scent or size, contact us within 30 days. We'll send the new item once we receive the original.

Weddings & Bulk Orders

Do you offer discounts for bulk orders?

Yes! We offer tiered discounts: 10% off 20-49 candles, 15% off 50-99 candles, and 20% off 100+ candles. Custom quotes available for orders over 500 units.

How far in advance should I place a wedding order?

We recommend placing wedding orders at least 6-8 weeks before your event date. This allows time for sampling, customization, and any adjustments. Rush orders may be accommodated for an additional fee.

Can I get samples before ordering in bulk?

Absolutely! We offer sample sets of our most popular scents for $25 (credited toward your bulk order). Contact our wholesale team to request samples.
