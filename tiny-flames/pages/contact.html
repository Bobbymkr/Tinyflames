Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US. Express options (1-2 days) are available at checkout. International orders typically arrive within 7-14 business days.

Can I customize my candle?

Yes! We offer custom labels where you can add names, dates, and short messages. Visit our Customize page to create your personalized candle.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unused candles in original packaging. Custom candles are final sale unless damaged. Contact us to initiate a return.

Do you offer bulk discounts for weddings?

Absolutely! We offer special pricing for orders of 20+ candles. Visit our Weddings & Corporate page or contact us directly for a custom quote.
